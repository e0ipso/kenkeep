Documentation in this repo is written for human readers: organized, structured, and short. Verbosity is the thing to cut, not structure.

The docs homepage and `README.md` carry the reader's attention, so they stay clear and enticing even when the rest of the docs shrink. The README's narrative hooks stay in place: the hero, the six slides, the why cards, and the packs teaser.

Rationale: the hooks are what keep a reader moving through the README. A shorter README that drops them is worse than a longer one that keeps them.
