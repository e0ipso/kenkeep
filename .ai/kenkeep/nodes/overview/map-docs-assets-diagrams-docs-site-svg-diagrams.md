Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the docs site live under `docs/assets/diagrams/`: `layout.svg`, `loop.svg`, `packs.svg`, `review-gate.svg`, and a `core/` subdirectory.

They follow the existing infographic house style: a cream background with ink linework, and the pink accent reserved for the steps the reader performs. Animated diagrams honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

The Mermaid charts in the internals pages stay as Mermaid rather than being redrawn as SVG.
